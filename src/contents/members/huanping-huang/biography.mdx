Dr. Huang’s research lies at the intersection of spatial data science and climate science, with a focus on understanding and predicting weather and climate extremes, such as extreme precipitation and hurricanes, in a changing climate. His work integrates large-scale geospatial and climate datasets, Earth system models, and advanced statistical methods to investigate the spatiotemporal patterns of extreme events, uncover their underlying physical mechanisms, and improve their prediction across different spatial and temporal scales.

His research also leverages spatial analytics and modeling to assess climate change impacts on human–environment systems. Through interdisciplinary approaches, his work aims to advance climate risk assessment, inform evidence-based decision-making, and strengthen societal resilience to environmental change.
