# SDSC joins the Stop the Stroke Hackathon, February 19, 2027

The **Spatial Data Science Center (SDSC)** is a partner in the **Stop the Stroke Hackathon**, a one-day design sprint at the **FSU Innovation Hub** on **Friday, February 19, 2027 (8 AM – 5 PM)**, run with **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**.

**[Event details and applications: innovation.fsu.edu/stopstroke](https://www.innovation.fsu.edu/stopstroke)**

## The challenge

Interdisciplinary student teams will work to develop solutions that reduce deaths from stroke. Teams will use the AWS Working Backwards innovation framework, real datasets, and the AWS Kiro agentic AI app-development platform, then pitch their prototypes to a panel of judges at the end of the day.

## SDSC's role

Where people live shapes their risk of dying from a stroke, and SDSC's contribution to the hackathon is built around that geography. The center's focus is on helping teams **map and analyze geographic variations in stroke-related death rates across the southeastern United States**, and understand how those patterns relate to the characteristics of the communities where they occur.

SDSC Director **A. Stewart Fotheringham** is one of the event's sponsors and experts, alongside colleagues from the College of Social Sciences and Public Policy, AWS, the FSU Career Center, and the Innovation Hub. SDSC researchers will be on hand throughout the day to help teams work with the spatial data.

## Who can take part

Students from all majors are encouraged to apply. Only **50 seats** are available, with priority given to social science students and to technology and engineering students.

Participants receive two meals and event t-shirts and giveaways, and teams compete for up to **$2,000 in cash prizes**. To be eligible for a prize, team members must take part in the entire design sprint.
