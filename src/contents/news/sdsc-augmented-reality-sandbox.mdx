# SDSC's augmented-reality sandbox is up and running

The **Spatial Data Science Center (SDSC)** has a new resident: an augmented-reality sandbox, now calibrated and running thanks to graduate research assistant **Chen-Lun Kao**.

It is a box of sand that knows its own shape. As you dig and pile the sand, the sandbox reads the surface and projects it straight back onto itself as colour-banded elevation with contour lines, redrawn as the sand moves. Hold a hand flat over the box and it starts to rain: the water runs downhill, follows the valleys, and collects in whichever basin is lowest.

The sandbox isn't a research instrument. It is the quickest way we know to make a contour map make sense. Build a hill and watch the lines crowd together on its steep side; dig a valley and see exactly where the rain goes.

If you are visiting SDSC, ask to see it, and bring your hands. To arrange a visit, [get in touch](/contact/).
