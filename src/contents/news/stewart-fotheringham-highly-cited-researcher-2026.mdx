# Stewart Fotheringham named a Highly Cited Researcher for 2026

SDSC Director **A. Stewart Fotheringham** has been named to **Clarivate's Highly Cited Researchers 2026** list, published on November 18, 2026. He is one of only **four Florida State University researchers** recognized this year. It is the second year in a row he has been named at FSU, after the 2025 list, and he was also recognized in 2023.

## A 1-in-1,000 recognition

Clarivate's Highly Cited Researchers list recognizes scientists and social scientists whose publications have had exceptional influence, both within their own fields and across the global research community. Everyone on the list has authored multiple Highly Cited Papers over the past eleven years: publications that rank in the **top 1% by citations** for their field and publication year in the Web of Science Core Collection.

Citations are the starting point but not the whole test. Clarivate refines the initial list with additional quantitative measures, qualitative analysis and expert judgment, as set out in its [selection process](https://clarivate.com/highly-cited-researchers/evaluation-and-selection/).

Of the world's scientists and social scientists, Highly Cited Researchers are **1 in 1,000**. The 2026 list names 6,802 researchers from more than 1,200 institutions in 62 countries and regions.

## The work behind it

Fotheringham is Krafft Professor of Spatial Data Science in the Department of Geography and a member of the U.S. National Academy of Sciences. His research analyzes spatial data with statistical, mathematical and computational methods. He is best known for his work on spatial interaction modeling and local statistical analysis, including geographically weighted regression (GWR) and its multiscale extension, MGWR.

Citations measure how often other researchers use and build on a body of work. For SDSC, the recognition shows how far the local modeling methods at the center of its research have traveled.

FSU covered his 2025 recognition in [Two Florida State University professors named among world's most highly cited researchers](https://artsandsciences.fsu.edu/node/15423). The full 2026 list is on [Clarivate's Highly Cited Researchers site](https://clarivate.com/highly-cited-researchers/).
