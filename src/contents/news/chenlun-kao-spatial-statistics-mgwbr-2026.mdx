# Chen-Lun Kao publishes new Spatial Statistics article on local spatial context

SDSC graduate research assistant **Chen-Lun Kao** has published a new article in _Spatial Statistics_, co-authored with **Mehak Sachdeva** and **A. Stewart Fotheringham**.

The paper, **"Uncovering Local Spatial Context via Multiscale Geographically Weighted Binomial Regression,"** appears in _Spatial Statistics_ **74, 101000**. The study advances multiscale geographically weighted binomial regression as a way to examine how local spatial context shapes binary outcomes, extending SDSC's work on local modeling and spatial heterogeneity.

For SDSC, the publication connects graduate student research, faculty collaboration, and continued methodological development in geographically weighted modeling.

Read the article through [Elsevier](https://authors.elsevier.com/a/1nA4D7su7AAYrb).
