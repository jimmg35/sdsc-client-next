# Stewart Fotheringham to visit Chongqing University and keynote Population Geography conference

SDSC Director **A. Stewart Fotheringham** will travel to China in **mid-June 2026** for two invited academic engagements.

At **Chongqing University**, he will give a presentation and receive an **Honorary Professorship**. From Chongqing, he will travel to **Nanjing** to deliver a keynote address at the **Population Geography Conference**.

The visit reflects continuing international recognition of Fotheringham's contributions to spatial analysis and provides another opportunity for SDSC's work in spatial data science to reach global geography audiences.
