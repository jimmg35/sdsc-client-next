The Spatial Data Science Center is pleased to announce the release of **MGWR 3.0**, the next major version of our free desktop software for calibrating multiscale geographically weighted regression models.

MGWR lets researchers explore how relationships between variables change across space, and at what spatial scale each process operates. It runs on both **Windows** and **macOS**, with no programming required.

**[Download MGWR 3.0 from the MGWR page](/mgwr)**

### Download notes

- **Windows:** the installer comes as a `.rar` archive. Extract it first, then run the setup program.
- **macOS:** the app comes as a `.zip` archive. Double-click it to extract, then open the app.

### Previous version

**MGWR 2.2.1** remains available for download on the [MGWR page](/mgwr#previous-version), for anyone who needs to reproduce earlier analyses or prefers to stay on that release.

### Citing MGWR

If you use MGWR in your work, please cite the references listed under [Citation references](/mgwr#citation-references) on the MGWR page.
