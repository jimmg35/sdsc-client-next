## The latest from SDSC

The window from **June 24 to September 24, 2026** carried a single story in the SDSC newsroom, so this is a shorter briefing than usual. The story earns the space on its own: national recognition for one of the center's graduate researchers.

[anchor:jacob-tagnan-usgif-2026-scholarship]
**Jacob N. Tagnan**, a Ph.D. student in the Department of Geography and a graduate research assistant with SDSC, has been named a **Doctoral Scholar** in the **United States Geospatial Intelligence Foundation's 2026 scholarship program**. USGIF made **24 awards** this cycle, reaching from rising college freshmen to doctoral candidates, and only **three** of them went to doctoral scholars.

The program has distributed more than **$2 million** to students at 153 colleges and universities since 2004, and it connects the people it funds to the wider geospatial intelligence community that USGIF convenes. For Tagnan, who works with **Mark W. Horner** in the Department of Geography, the award recognizes the doctoral research and gives it a place in that professional network.

For SDSC, the award matters because of who it reaches. The recognition comes from a national foundation outside the university, and it goes to one of the center's graduate researchers for their own doctoral work. It is a single story rather than a trend, and it is reported here at that scale.
